Livelihood & Entrepreneurship

Sangati Shoppe (संगTea) accessible street vendor carts and artisan product showcases for economic independence.

EXACT PROGRAMME RECORD
"Sangati Shoppe is a mobile, accessible street vendor's cart — संगTea — designed so that a person with disability can run a business independently and with dignity. We have partnered with Evoo on VentureonWheels carts, and worked with NHFDC on handmade leather goods, file folders, wallets and decorative items made by our community."
KEY METRICIndependent Micro-Businesses
KEY METRICNHFDC Handicraft Partner
KEY METRICVentureonWheels Cart

Key Achievements & Deliverables

  • संगTea / Sangati Shoppe: Custom ergonomic vendor cart for independent micro-entrepreneurs
  • VentureonWheels partnership with Evoo for mobile cart deployments
  • Handmade leather items, folders, wallets, and decor crafted by Divyang artisans
  • Exhibition support at Divyakala Mela with NHFDC
