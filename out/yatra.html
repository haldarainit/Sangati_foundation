THE CHRONICLE OF THE EXPEDITION

From Delhi to Dhanushkodi & Back

Sangati Foundation announced a cross-country ride beginning 15 December 2024, from Delhi to the Sri Lanka border at Dhanushkodi, Rameshwaram. Founder Sudhir Dhir led a team of five people with disability driving modified vehicles.

The flag-off was on Saturday 14 December from Modern School, Barakhamba Road, New Delhi, by Padma Shri Dr Deepa Malik. She charged up everyone — her presence alone was all the inspiration we could have wished for.

The Yatra reached Hyderabad on 29 December, hosted by the Ideabytes team. It had been full of challenges and difficulties, and the team overcame them with grit and a practical, solution-finding approach.

They did it. The longest ride ever undertaken by Divyang themselves to highlight both the problems and the solutions concluded on 3 January 2025, in a cold, foggy Delhi winter. There are no accolades enough to express even a fraction of what they achieved.
