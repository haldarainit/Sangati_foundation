Get Involved

Join hands with Sangati as a volunteer, corporate partner, or donor-in-kind.

Ways You Can Contribute

1. Volunteer

We welcome passionate individuals across India to contribute skills in accessibility auditing, sign language interpretation, content writing, event management, and tech mentorship.

OPPORTUNITIES:
  • Road & Public Space Accessibility Auditor
  • Project Udaan Coding & Tech Mentor
  • Sign Language Interpreter & Accessibility Specialist
  • Event Support for Sangati Yatra & Marathon Runs
Apply to Volunteer

2. Corporate Partner

Partner with Sangati through CSR initiatives to fund accessible public toilets, sponsor Project Udaan batches, or retrofit mobility equipment.

PARTNERSHIP BENEFITS:
  • 80G Tax Exemption Certificates for all CSR contributions
  • Measurable ESG & DE&I impact reporting
  • Joint branding on accessible public infrastructure and mobile carts
  • Accessibility & Sensitivity workshops for corporate employees
Explore Partnership

3. Donate-in-Kind

We accept functional assistive devices, medical supplies, and tech equipment to support our rural health centers and training labs.

NEEDED ITEMS:
  • Laptops, desktop PCs, monitors, and repair toolkits for training labs
  • Wheelchairs (manual & motorized), crutches, walkers, and folding ramps
  • Medical supplies: oximeters, BP monitors, thermometers, sanitizers, and diagnostic kits
  • Braille displays, screen readers, and adaptive keyboards
