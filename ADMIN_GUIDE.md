# Sangati Foundation — Admin Panel Guide

This is the guide for whoever updates the website. **You do not need any technical
knowledge and you do not need to contact a developer** to change text, add a
story, or upload photos.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://your-website-address/studio**
2. Sign in with Google, GitHub, or your email address.

   You will only be able to sign in if you have been invited to the project.
   To invite someone else, go to **sanity.io/manage → Members → Invite**.

Bookmark that `/studio` address. It is the admin panel for the whole site.

---

## How the panel is laid out

The menu on the left has two kinds of entries:

**Lists you can add to** — click the ✏️ pencil / "+" button to create a new item:

| Menu item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **News & Posters** | The posters archive. The quickest thing to publish. |
| **Stories** | Long-form stories, each with its own page. |
| **Programmes** | The six programmes, each with its own page and photo gallery. |
| **Team & Trustees** | Trustee profiles and leadership cards on the About page. |

**Pages you edit** — these exist once and cannot be deleted:

| Menu item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Homepage** | The big rotating slider, the four counting numbers, "Who We Are", the Yatra teaser, and the photo gallery. |
| **About Page** | Page title, brand idea, founding poem, timeline preview. |
| **Impact Page** | Headline figures and the year-by-year timeline. |
| **Yatra Page** | The campaign story and every stop on the route map. |
| **Donate Page** | Donation amounts and what each one funds. |

---

## The one rule: click Publish

Your changes are **not live** until you click the **Publish** button at the
bottom of the screen.

Everything is saved as a draft automatically as you type, so you can never lose
work — but the public website only shows published content. After you publish,
the change appears on the website within about a minute.

---

## Adding a news poster

1. Click **News & Posters** → the **+** button at the top.
2. Fill in the Title, upload the Poster image, and set the Date and Category.
3. Write a Description — one or two sentences.
4. Set **Position** to control where it appears (1 is first).
5. Click **Publish**.

## Writing a story

1. Click **Stories** → the **+** button.
2. Type the Title, then click **Generate** next to "Web address".
3. Upload a Cover photo, fill in Category, Date and the Short summary.
4. Write the story in **Full story**. Press Enter for a new paragraph. Use the
   toolbar for headings, **bold**, links, bullet lists, and to drop in photos.
5. Click **Publish**.

⚠️ Once a story is published, avoid changing its "Web address" — anyone who
shared the old link would get a "page not found".

## Changing the homepage slider

1. Click **Homepage** → the **Homepage slider** tab.
2. Click **Add item** for a new slide, or click an existing one to edit it.
3. Drag slides by the handle on the left to reorder them.

The Sangati logo intro animation always plays first and is not in this list.

## Replacing a photo

Click the photo, then **Replace**. After uploading, click the **crop icon** and
drag the circle over the most important part of the picture — that is the part
that stays visible when the photo is cropped on a phone.

---

## Please always fill in the caption / alt text

Every photo has a **Caption / alt text** box. This is read aloud to blind
visitors using screen readers.

For a website built for persons with disability, leaving it blank is the one
mistake worth avoiding. One plain sentence describing what is in the photo is
enough.

---

## Made a mistake?

- **Undo before publishing** — click **Discard changes** at the bottom.
- **Undo after publishing** — open the document, click the **⋯** menu at the top
  right, choose **Review changes**, and restore an earlier version. Sanity keeps
  a full history.
- **Delete something** — open it and choose **Delete** from the **⋯** menu.
  (Pages like the Homepage cannot be deleted, only edited.)

---

## What you cannot change here

The admin panel controls **content**. It does not control page layout, colours,
fonts, animations, the navigation menu structure, or the creation of entirely
new page types. Those still need a developer.

Contact and organisation details (helpline, addresses, bank and 80G details) are
also deliberately kept in the code rather than the panel, because they rarely
change and a typo there is costly.
